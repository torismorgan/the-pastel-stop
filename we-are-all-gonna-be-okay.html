Dear Her / 001

we are
all gonna
be okay

For the days when life feels like it’s moving and you’re somehow standing still.

Life Lately26 September 20265 min read

Dear her,

I know there are days when you feel like you’re not enough.

Days when it feels like you’re on a treadmill. You’re putting in all this effort, going as fast as you can, only to look around and realize you’re still in the exact same spot.

I know because I feel it too.

All the midnight candles. All the burning out. All the trying, the crying, the setbacks, the tiny wins that somehow come with another roadblock right behind them.

Sometimes I sit down and genuinely wonder: what is life?

Because seriously. What is it?

Are we all just here to do the same thing? Wake up. Eat. Work. Sleep. Repeat. And eventually… die.

Yeah. I went there.

There are days when I desperately wish someone could just tell me what the end goal is. Give me the cheat sheet. Tell me that if I do A, B and C, everything will work out exactly the way it’s supposed to.

But you know what they say: man plans, and God laughs. Or, in my case, holds my passport until the deadline and makes me miss a flight to one of the most important conferences of my life.

Okay, TMI there.

Anyways… I wish this were the part where I told you that everything is going to be fine.

But everything isn’t always going to be fine.

There will be disappointment. There will be pain. There will be loss. There will be plans that don’t work out, people who don’t stay, and things you wanted so badly that simply weren’t meant for you.

Unfortunately, that’s life too.

On the days you feel behind

And then there are those days when you look at yourself and think, God, I’m such a disappointment.

You remember the 12-year-old version of you who thought she’d be living the Kylie Jenner life by 21.

Or the 14-year-old who was absolutely convinced that by 28 she’d have her entire life figured out. A million-dollar company. A house. Two cars. Vacations twice a year. Dabble in a man or two here and there, since we’re talking.

Just kidding. Obviously.

Girl…

She had no idea what was going on.

Neither do we, apparently.

And sometimes you look in the mirror and don’t even recognize the person looking back at you. You wonder when you became her. You think about everything you thought your life would be by now and everything it isn’t.

What I want you to remember

But I want you to remember something else too.

Remember all those other times you were convinced the world was ending.

The days you couldn’t imagine surviving another week. The moments when you just wanted everything to stop.

And somehow—
another second passed.
Then another minute.
Then another day.
Then another year.

And somewhere between all of that, you laughed again.

You look back now at some of the things that once felt so big, so impossible, so this is literally going to ruin my life, and somehow they don’t feel quite as big anymore.

It’s almost funny. Not because they didn’t matter. They did. But because you had no idea that one day, they’d just be something you made it through.

And isn’t that crazy?

Because how many things feel that big right now? How many things are keeping you up at night, making you question everything, feeling like they’ll never end?

Maybe one day you’ll look back at those too and think, God, I really thought that was going to break me.

And then life happened anyway.

You saw a ridiculous sunset and stopped to take a picture. Your friend said something stupid and you laughed until your stomach hurt. A new episode of your favourite K-drama aired. You found a song you played seventeen times in one day.

You got your matcha order — sweet cream, oat milk, two pumps of vanilla syrup, strawberry purée at the bottom, strawberry cold foam — and your tongue danced for joy the moment it touched.

And for a second, life was good again.

Not perfect, but good.

I think that’s what I’m starting to understand.

Life changes. Shit happens. Plans fail.

Some days life works in your favour. Some days it looks you dead in the eye and says, absolutely not.

But somehow, we keep making it to another day.

And maybe that’s the part we don’t give ourselves enough credit for.

We made it here.

After every version of our life we thought would destroy us, we’re still here. Still finding things to laugh about. Still taking pictures of pretty skies. Still loving people. Still making plans, even after learning that plans don’t always work out. Still finding tiny reasons to think—

Oh.
Life is actually kind of beautiful.

So no, I can’t promise you that everything is going to be fine.

But I think I can promise you something else.

We’re going to be okay.

And okay might not always look the way we imagined it would.

Sometimes okay is thriving. Sometimes okay is starting over. Sometimes okay is crying in your room and getting up the next morning anyway, putting on some makeup so nobody notices the puffy eyes. Sometimes okay is simply making it to tomorrow.

But whatever version of okay finds us—
I think we’ll find our way there.

We always have.

From,

the girl who made it to another day.
