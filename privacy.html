Legal / Privacy

privacy policy.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Your privacy matters to us, and we've kept this policy in plain language. Here's what happens with your information when you visit or use The Pastel Stop.

1. Who we are

The Pastel Stop (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a lifestyle and community brand based in Lethbridge, Alberta, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through thepastelstop.com (the “site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

We keep this to the minimum we need. Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Your email address, if you join our list through the “Join the Stop” sign-up.
  • Stories you share on The Pause Files: the text you write, what you'd like from the community (advice, a second opinion, or to vent), and your confirmation that we may share it anonymously. We don't ask for your name or email with a story. Please don't include names or identifying details.
  • Technical information that any website receives when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you view. Our hosting provider processes this to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Small bits of browser storage that remember whether you've already seen or dismissed our sign-up pop-up, or already signed up, so we don't keep showing it. These are not used for advertising or tracking you across other sites.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and Google receives your IP address.

3. How we use your information

  • To send the emails you asked for, like letters, events and updates from The Pastel Stop.
  • To read, moderate and, with your permission, publish stories anonymously on The Pause Files.
  • To run, secure and improve the site, and to respond when you contact us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Stories and anonymity

Stories are shared on The Pause Files without a name. We may lightly edit a story for clarity, length or to remove identifying details, and we may decide not to publish it. Because a story is published for the community to read, please keep out names, workplaces, schools, locations or anything that could identify you or someone else. If a story you sent is published and you want it changed or removed, contact us at [contact email] and describe the story so we can find it.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as needed for their service:

  • Hosting and security: Cloudflare.
  • Email sign-ups and newsletters: [email service provider].
  • Story submissions: [form service provider].

Some links on our site, such as books we recommend, are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon sets its own cookies and handles your information under its own privacy notice once you leave our site.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights, safety and security of our community. These providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States.

6. How long we keep it

We keep email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We keep submitted stories until you ask us to remove them or we no longer need them. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security and performance.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send.
  • Access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent for emails or for a story to be shared, at any time.

Depending on where you live, including Canada, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and California, you may have additional legal rights over your personal information. We'll respond to reasonable requests within the time the law requires.

8. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we'll make it clear on the site.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at [contact email].
